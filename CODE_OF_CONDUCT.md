# Code of conduct

This is a small, public space for talking about a vector editor. The rules are short.

Be decent to people. Critique the software, not the person reporting on it. Assume the person on
the other end is doing something reasonable that you have not thought of yet.

Not welcome here: harassment, slurs, sexual attention, posting someone's private information, and
sustained hostility after being asked to stop.

I moderate this repo myself. If something crosses a line, email **support@subpath.dev** rather than
arguing it out in a thread. I will edit, lock, or delete comments and block accounts when it is
warranted, and I will not always explain the call publicly.
